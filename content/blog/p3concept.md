## Overall Project Concept
1. **1.Thesis: Nonverbal Communication**

- For our group's project we wanted to explore the meta side of communcation. We really latched on the idea of how alien's would communicate with one another both verbally and nonverbally. With non-verbal communication in mind, we wanted to explore how much of how we think is interlinked with each other without having to verbally confirm. Our shared perspectives and classifciaitons of things. </br>

2. **2.Design Concept**
- With that thought in mind we used the inspiration of the desktop app Obsidian and it mapping feature as basis for our project. We want to create a sort of library / archive that has two interfaces. The typical catalog you see with other archives and the "neuro" interface. To see how much of how we think and communicated is connected on a deeper level.
